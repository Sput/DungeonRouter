# Campaign-note retrieval test queries

Upload the three neighboring `.md` files through DungeonRouter's **Add note** control. Ask the following questions in Auto mode and confirm that answers display one or more **Campaign** source cards.

## Direct retrieval

1. **What phrase opens the Moon Gate?**
   - Expected: `Silver remembers the first dawn` and a campaign-note citation.
2. **Who leads the Gilded Choir?**
   - Expected: Precentor Sable Quill.
3. **What is Heroic Recovery?**
   - Expected: the once-per-long-rest Hit Die rule, including its restrictions.
4. **At what hit point total does Varkesh retreat?**
   - Expected: below 45 hit points.

## Cross-note synthesis

5. **How can the party convince Mara Venn to help them open the Moon Gate?**
   - Expected: connect Mara's hidden star-iron with Corin's journal and the promise to free the miners.
6. **Give me a plan for entering Emberglass Keep and rescuing the miners.**
   - Expected: combine the low-tide road or eastern aqueduct, sluice controls, foundry location, and possible surrender conditions.
7. **What information does Corin have that the Gilded Choir wants, and why?**
   - Expected: connect the Moon Gate phrase or stolen ledger with the Choir's search for the Observatory's eruption-prediction machine.

## Campaign facts versus official rules

8. **Can Varkesh fly while fighting inside Emberglass Keep?**
   - Expected: no, because of the campaign-specific wound and tunnel constraint. The answer should label this as a campaign fact even though a standard young red dragon has a fly speed.
9. **When a character drops to 0 hit points, can they use Heroic Recovery instead of falling unconscious?**
   - Expected: explain the table-specific rule and distinguish it from normal SRD rules.
10. **Does fire damage stop Heroic Recovery? What about radiant damage?**
    - Expected: fire does not prevent it; radiant damage does.

## Negative control

11. **What is the password to the Sapphire Library?**
    - Expected: the answer should not invent a campaign fact. No uploaded note defines a Sapphire Library or its password.

## Citation checks

For every positive case, verify that:

- at least one source card is labeled **Campaign**;
- clicking the citation opens the passage containing the claimed fact;
- the answer does not attach an SRD citation to a campaign-only fact;
- unsupported citation warnings do not appear.
