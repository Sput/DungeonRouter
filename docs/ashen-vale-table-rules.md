# Ashen Vale: Table Rules and Encounter Notes

## Heroic Recovery

This campaign uses a table-specific rule called **Heroic Recovery**. Once per long rest, a player character reduced to 0 hit points may immediately spend one Hit Die. The character regains hit points equal to the roll plus its Constitution modifier and falls prone.

Heroic Recovery is not an action or reaction. It cannot be used if the character was reduced to 0 hit points by radiant damage or while inside an antimagic field.

## Emberglass Foundry

The eastern foundry contains three iron catwalks suspended 20 feet above a lava channel. At initiative count 20, one unoccupied catwalk chosen by the DM becomes unstable. A creature that ends its next turn on that catwalk must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or fall into a maintenance net 10 feet below.

Four Gilded Choir guards watch the six captive miners. The guards surrender if Precentor Sable Quill is defeated or if the party displays Corin Venn's translated ledger.

## The Cinder Wyrm

The Cinder Wyrm is a young red dragon named **Varkesh**. Unlike an ordinary young red dragon, Varkesh cannot fly inside Emberglass Keep because an old harpoon wound prevents him from fully extending his left wing in the tunnels.

Varkesh is immune to fire, fears magical cold, and retreats if reduced below 45 hit points. He knows the location of the Observatory of Tides but does not know how to open the Moon Gate.
