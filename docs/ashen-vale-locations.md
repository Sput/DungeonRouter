# Ashen Vale: Locations and Secrets

## The Moon Gate

The Moon Gate is a circular arch of black glass standing in the ruins north of Briarwatch. It opens only when the phrase **"Silver remembers the first dawn"** is spoken while a piece of star-iron touches the center rune.

The gate remains open for three minutes. It leads to the Observatory of Tides, not to another plane. Crossing the gate while carrying an open flame causes the traveler to arrive in the observatory's flooded lower chamber instead of its entrance hall.

## Briarwatch

Briarwatch is a fortified village built around an enormous petrified tree. The village reeve is **Mara Venn**, a retired cartographer who wears a brass compass with a cracked blue face.

Mara knows that the old road to Emberglass Keep is safe only at low tide. She will share her map if the party returns the journal of her missing brother, Corin Venn.

## Emberglass Keep

Emberglass Keep sits inside the caldera of Mount Vael. Its western entrance is sealed, but a narrow aqueduct enters beneath the eastern wall. The aqueduct is passable by Medium creatures only after the sluice controls in Briarwatch are closed.

The phrase **"the crown drinks last"** disables the keep's brass guardian for one minute. This phrase is carved beneath Mara Venn's cracked compass lid.
