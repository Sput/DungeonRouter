# Ashen Vale: People and Factions

## Mara Venn

Mara Venn is the reeve of Briarwatch and a former member of the Lantern Survey. She is direct, patient, and suspicious of anyone carrying symbols of the Gilded Choir. Her immediate goal is to recover her brother Corin's journal.

Mara secretly possesses the only known piece of star-iron in Briarwatch. It is hidden inside her cracked blue compass. She does not know the words that activate the Moon Gate.

## Corin Venn

Corin Venn disappeared while exploring the salt caves below Emberglass Keep. He is alive and sheltering in the **Chapel of Cinders**, where he is translating a ledger stolen from the Gilded Choir.

Corin's journal contains the phrase that opens the Moon Gate: **"Silver remembers the first dawn."** He will give the journal to anyone who promises to help free the miners held beneath Emberglass Keep.

## The Gilded Choir

The Gilded Choir is led by **Precentor Sable Quill**. The faction is collecting star-iron because Sable believes the Observatory of Tides contains a machine that can predict volcanic eruptions.

The Choir publicly claims to protect Briarwatch. In reality, it holds six miners in the keep's eastern foundry and forces them to excavate beneath the caldera.

Choir members recognize each other by the response **"And the ember answers"** when someone says **"Who keeps the final flame?"**
